Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Overview

Atlas is a personal assistant project operated by its owner. This policy explains how information may be accessed when Google services are connected to Atlas.

Information accessed

If authorised by the account holder, Atlas may access Google Calendar information such as event details and schedules to support calendar synchronisation and personal scheduling. The information accessed depends on the permissions granted.

How information is used

Calendar information is used to provide the personal assistant features requested by the account holder, such as scheduling and calendar-aware planning.

Storage and security

Information and authentication credentials may be processed or stored by the systems used to operate Atlas. Appropriate safeguards should be maintained to protect credentials and personal information from unauthorised access.

Sharing

Personal information is not intentionally made public through this website. Information handling may depend on the services and integrations configured by the account holder.

Data retention and deletion

The account holder can revoke Atlas's Google account access through their Google Account security settings. Copies of information stored by Atlas may need to be deleted separately.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of this personal assistant project through the contact method provided by them.
